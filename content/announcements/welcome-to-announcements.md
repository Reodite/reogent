You can now find Announcements at the top of Unity's navigation.

## Read an update

Scroll through the cards to browse announcements, with the newest entries first. Open a card to read the full post on its own page.

Each post has a direct link, so you can bookmark an update or share it with someone who uses Reodite. Use the back arrow beside the title to return to the feed.
